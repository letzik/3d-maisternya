// 3D modeling club · 2026–2027

See the shape.
Build worlds.

The learning resource of the “3D Workshop” club. We don’t learn one program — we learn to see simple shapes in any object and to assemble our own things from them: characters, vehicles, buildings, game props.

Start with the first stepYear map

This resource is bilingual: the language switch at the top opens the same page in the other language.

The idea in three steps

01

See

Any object is made of simple shapes: box, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, plane. This is the skill we train all year.

02

Break down

A complex idea is not built as one solid piece. Split it into 3–5 parts, work on each separately and assemble them at the end.

03

Assemble

Every block ends with a real artifact you can show: from your first colored object to a model in AR on your phone.

Year map

Ten blocks — from finding your way around the interface to a portfolio page. Each one ends with a checkpoint and has a “challenge” for those who move faster.

What you need

  • A computerAn ordinary PC or laptop is enough.
  • BlenderFree 3D software. We work in Blender 5.2 LTS.
  • A phoneFor block 8: your own model in augmented reality. iOS or Android.
  • Your own ideaA character, vehicle, building — whatever interests you. No experience needed.
