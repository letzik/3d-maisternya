Exercises

Short interactive exercises run right in the browser: nothing to install, no sign-up, no tracking.

The exercises are designed for 5–15 minutes and complement the sessions. Each one is tied to a block of the program: the exercise page shows exactly where it helps and which terms are worth knowing.

  • Trainers (See the Shape, Clean Mesh) open full-screen and work on a computer and on a phone. Your score and streak are stored only in your browser.
  • Templates (the AR showcase) are ready-made pages that you copy and fill with your own model.
  • You can change an exercise’s language in its top corner; by default it matches the site’s language.
