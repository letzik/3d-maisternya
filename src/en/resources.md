## Software and tools

- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/) — free 3D modeling software. We work in Blender 5.2 LTS ([release notes](https://www.blender.org/press/blender-5-2-lts-release/)).
- [model-viewer](https://modelviewer.dev/) — Google’s web component that shows a GLB model in the browser and opens AR on a phone ([[block:8|block 8]]).
- Any free QR code generator — to link to your published AR page. Generate the code only for the final `https://…` address.
- Any quick GLB viewer (in the browser or local) — to check the export from [[block:7|block 7]].

## Sources of the methods

The club’s program builds on the following materials. Most are in English; we learn Blender’s terms in English anyway, so this is good practice.

- **Imphenzia, Workshops** — a five-hotkey blockout and face colors without UV unwrapping: [imphenzia.com/workshops](https://imphenzia.com/workshops). For [[block:1]].
- **Blender Guru, Donut tutorial** — one small object goes through the full cycle; every step is saved as a separate file: [blenderguru.com](https://www.blenderguru.com/posts/blender-donut-v5-tutorial). For [[block:1]] and [[block:5]].
- **GameDev.tv / Grant Abbitt, Blender Low Poly Characters** — a quick character build-out and references: [gamedev.tv](https://gamedev.tv/courses/blender-low-poly-characters). For [[block:3]] and [[block:5]].
- **Grant Abbitt, Easy Rigging Using Rigify** — a 15-minute repeatable rigging sequence: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdTuAY23vzk). For [[block:6]].
- **CG Cookie, 8 Tips for Clean Topology in Blender** — edge flow through simple shapes, the MatCap check: [blog.cgcookie.com](https://blog.cgcookie.com/posts/guide-to-clean-topology/). For [[block:4]].
- **StraySpark, Blender 5.x to Unreal Engine 5.7 (2026)** — an up-to-date pipeline checklist: [strayspark.studio](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/blender-5-to-unreal-engine-57-asset-pipeline-2026). For [[block:7]].
- **Google for Developers, AR with model-viewer** — Quick Look and Scene Viewer from a web page: [developers.google.com](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/webxr/model-viewer). For [[block:8]].
- **Project-based learning** — why learning through your own project works better: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project-based_learning).
