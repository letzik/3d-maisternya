## What the club is

“3D Workshop” is a modeling and 3D-printing club. We follow the whole path from sketch to finished object: creating our own 3D models, preparing them for display or printing and making the objects. The format is theory and practice, individual and team work; at the end of the year there is an exhibition and demo day of the finished projects.

The club is open to college students and to anyone who wants to learn: no experience is needed, and the age range in the project plan is 15 to 35.

## The new direction for 2026–2027

This year the club deliberately changes course:

- **Less CAD and printing for the sake of souvenirs.** Students already study CAD in other subjects, and printing stays as the final realization of the best projects.
- **More polygonal modeling** — this is what participants actually ask for: game characters and assets. Next in the program: topology, preparing models for game engines (Unreal Engine, Unity) and your own AR showcase.
- **The main goal is not the software but the skill:** seeing simple shapes in any object, breaking the complex into simple parts and sharpening the basics so much that moving to another editor is no problem.
- **Self-expression comes first.** Everyone works on their own idea; the leader adapts the difficulty.

## About this site

- **Bilingual.** The language switch at the top opens the same page in Ukrainian or English; your choice is remembered.
- **No sign-up, no tracking.** Your progress (“checkpoint done”) and exercise scores are stored only in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
- **Static and open.** The site is made of ordinary HTML pages and runs on GitHub Pages; the source code and instructions for changing it are in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/letzik/3d-maisternya).
- **A place for your projects.** At the end of the year this is where participants’ portfolio pages and AR showcases will appear ([[block:9|block 9]]).
